- Round 1, ESCALATE: MIN1 [minor] Concerns the approved requirements: ADR 018 allows hand-authored committed SVG where the requirements say interface artwork is repository-generated. The approved requirements say 'repository-generated SVG' under 'Visual asset classes' and 'repository-generated interface artwork' under 'Non-goals', while ADR 018 decision 2, the verified display forms, I30, and F123 also allow an SVG source authored for this project and committed under site/assets/ with author and licence recorded (the natural form of a project mark). The planning cannot widen the approved wording on its own, and the two readings lead to materially different work: restricting to build-time generation means the project mark and the course-area illustrations are drawn by Python code, which is feasible but unusual for a logotype; allowing an authored source needs the owner to amend the requirements wording. Decision 37 itself only says 'visual identity and navigation, no scientific evidence claim' and the change request says 'Decorative SVG can establish identity', so the planner's recommendation is to amend the requirements to 'SVG whose generating code or authored source, with its author and licence, is in the repository; never a raster, stock, copied, or externally hosted image', which keeps the purpose of the rule (every asset traceable to a reviewed source in the repository, nothing generated committed, nothing external). If the owner prefers the strict reading, the planner will restrict ADR 018, I30, the display forms, and F123 to build-time generation in the next revision.
